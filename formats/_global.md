# Global rules (applied to every format)

- Infamazing is a faceless YouTube Shorts channel: food, candy, toy, fast food, theme park and pop culture trivia.
- Output is ONE single paragraph. No title, no headers, no labels, no markdown, no line breaks, no notes.
- No em dashes. Plain, spoken, human-sounding language a narrator reads out loud.
- Every fact must be verified with web search before it goes in the script. Use real names, years and numbers.
  If sources disagree or a claim is only company lore, hedge it ("reportedly", "according to the company").
  Never invent a figure. If the premise of the idea turns out to be wrong, rewrite the angle around what is true.
- Emotion over dry facts: surprise, outrage, "no way" moments. Every sentence pushes toward the reveal.
- Numbers are written the way they are spoken in a VO ("45 million dollars", "1962").
- Never end with a moral or a summary of what was said.
