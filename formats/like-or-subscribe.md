# Story reveal + "Like if A, subscribe if B"
Newer longer format: shocking claim first, story told in "but / so" chains, a relevant two-sided CTA at peak tension, then the payoff and a punchline.
chars: 1100-1250

Structure, as one paragraph (about 200-215 words):
1. Open with the shocking claim itself, stated as fact ("Hawaiian pizza has absolutely nothing to do with Hawaii and was actually invented in Canada by...").
2. Tell the real story with names, places and years. Keep it moving with "But", "So", "And".
3. At peak tension, BEFORE the final reveal, one relevant two-sided CTA: "Like if <side A>, subscribe if <side B>." Both sides must be about this topic.
4. The payoff: the part that makes it crazy, with the real number or outcome.
5. A closing punchline line that reframes everything (often "So a ... who ... became ...").

Example:
Burger King is not called Burger King in Australia and the reason why involves a pancake mix and a 45 million dollar lawsuit. In 1971, an entrepreneur named Jack Cowin bought the rights to bring Burger King to Australia, but when he went to register the name, he discovered a small takeaway shop in Adelaide had already trademarked it years earlier. So Cowin had to pick a different name from a list of brands owned by Burger King's parent company Pillsbury, and the one he chose was Hungry Jack's, which was originally just a pancake mix from the baking aisle of an American grocery store. Like if you've eaten at Hungry Jack's, subscribe if you had no idea it was Burger King. Cowin built Hungry Jack's into a massive chain with over 400 locations across Australia. But in the late 1990s, when the original trademark expired, Burger King's corporate owners tried to reclaim the country by opening their own stores and pushing Cowin out. So Cowin sued, and the Australian courts sided with him, awarding Hungry Jack's 45 million dollars in damages. To this day, every location in Australia is still called Hungry Jack's, and most Australians have no idea they've been eating Burger King their entire lives.
