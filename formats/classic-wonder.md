# Classic "Have you ever wondered"
The original Infamazing formula: wonder hook, "you might think" setup, 3-option like/subscribe/heart CTA, reveal, "how many times did I say X?"
chars: 750-825

Structure, in this exact order, as one paragraph:
1. "Have you ever wondered <the question>?"
2. "You might think <the obvious assumption>, but when you hear <the real thing> it might surprise you because this is not <an/any> ordinary <thing>."
3. "So <restate the question>?"
4. "But first, a quick question. Which <category item> would you <verb> forever? <Option A>, leave a like. <Option B>, subscribe. <Option C>, comment a heart. If you love all <category> do all three."
5. "So <the verified reveal with real years / numbers / names>." 2-4 sentences.
6. One punchy closing line that reframes the fact.
7. "So, how many times did I say <the keyword>?"  (the keyword is the brand / thing named most in the script)

Works for veins: which came first, which is more expensive, which is more dangerous, which is faster,
which is spicier, whatever happened to, single-topic reveals.

Example:
Have you ever wondered which cookie came first, Oreo or Hydrox? You might think Oreo invented the chocolate sandwich cookie, but when you hear the real timeline it might surprise you because this is not any ordinary snack. So which cookie is the real original? But first, a quick question. Which cookie would you dunk forever? Oreo, leave a like. Chips Ahoy, subscribe. Nutter Butter, comment a heart. If you love all cookies do all three. So Hydrox launched in 1908, four full years before Oreo even existed. Nabisco copied the chocolate wafer and cream design directly and released Oreo in 1912. Better marketing and a catchier name let Oreo crush its own inspiration until most people assumed Hydrox was the copycat. The world's best selling cookie started out as the imitation. So, how many times did I say Oreo?
