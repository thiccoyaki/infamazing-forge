# Historic Foods escalation
Historic Foods style: "Today I learned / Throwback to / In <year>" opener, brand mistake or dark lore, escalating "but" chain, a dry one-line joke at the end.
chars: 500-800

Structure, as one paragraph:
1. Opener with the whole hook in one sentence: "In 2016, McDonald's launched one of the dumbest contests ever when..." or "<Food> was considered poison to eat for centuries because it had one flaw ... until <unexpected person> <did something> by accident."
2. "Because ..." explains the setup in plain words.
3. Escalate with "But ...", "And ...", "So ..." beats, each bigger than the last, with real numbers / names / years.
4. The outcome (what it cost, what changed, what still happens today).
5. Optional: one dry, deadpan joke line in first person ("Bro was so good at cooking he accidentally cooked himself.").

Example:
In the 1990s, Little Caesars didn't care anymore and accidentally started a giant pizza war. Because when Little Caesars couldn't dominate on price and speed alone, they released a massive 22 inch pizza called the Big Big Cheese for just 8.88 dollars. It was successful for a while until Pizza Hut saw that and released a bigger 24 inch pizza called the Bigfoot. But when Domino's saw that, they released an even bigger 30 inch pizza called the Dominator, and many other pizza chains felt forced to join the giant pizza battle. But everyone kept making bigger and bigger pizzas until they realized they were losing money and the deliveries were becoming a nightmare. And eventually they all quietly discontinued the giant pizzas and went back to making normal ones.
